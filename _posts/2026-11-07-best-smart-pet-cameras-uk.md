## What to Consider Before Buying an Urban Pet Camera

1. **Storage Costs:** If you prefer avoiding ongoing monthly cloud fees, look for cameras offering local microSD card slots like the Tapo series.
2. **Coverage Area:** A fixed-angle camera works fine placed in a corner pointing at a crate or bed, while pan-and-tilt models are necessary to follow active pets moving across open-plan rooms.
3. **Placement & Mounting:** Compact plug-in models keep kitchen counters clear and take up zero floor space in smaller city flats.

## Final Verdict
For most apartment owners looking for high resolution, pan-and-tilt movement, and zero monthly subscriptions, the **Tapo C211** offers the best overall performance and value. If you already use Alexa devices throughout your home and prefer integrated privacy features, the **Ring Indoor Camera** is a solid option.
