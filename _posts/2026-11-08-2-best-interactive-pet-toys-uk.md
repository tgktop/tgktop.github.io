## 3. Buying Guide: Choosing Interactive Toys for Urban Flats

Before buying interactive pet toys for apartment living, consider these three important factors.

### 1. Noise Levels

Noise can become an issue in flats, particularly where rooms are close together or neighbours share walls.

Electronic toys such as the GoldenSun may produce mechanical sounds during operation. Consider where you intend to position the toy and whether your pet is comfortable with moving components.

For quieter enrichment, a puzzle such as the Nina Ottosson offers an alternative without motors or electronic movement.

### 2. Storage and Available Space

Floor space is valuable in smaller UK apartments.

Look for toys that are easy to move, store and clean. Multi-purpose toys can be particularly useful because they provide different activities without requiring several separate products.

The GoldenSun combines three play modes in one unit, while the Nina Ottosson puzzle can be put away when enrichment time is finished.

### 3. Connectivity and Power Requirements

Not every product marketed as a smart or interactive pet toy includes Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or smartphone connectivity.

The GoldenSun uses a rechargeable battery and automatic timers rather than requiring a connected smart-home setup.

The Nina Ottosson puzzle is entirely manual and requires no electricity.

Think about whether you actually need app connectivity or whether simple automatic operation and traditional enrichment are enough for your household.
