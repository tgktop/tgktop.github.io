## Key Buying Factors for Urban Pet Feeders

1. **Space & Footprint:** Compact vertical hoppers (like PetLibro) take up minimal floor space in small kitchens compared to wide twin-bowl setups.
2. **Food Type:** Ensure you match the feeder to your pet's diet—standard hoppers only handle dry kibble, while rotated disc models (like Cat Mate or PetLibro Polar) are needed for wet or fresh food.
3. **Power Reliability:** Look for units featuring battery backups so your pet never misses a meal during brief power interruptions.
